Before the plan

The higher you lead, the less you're told.

Seniority filters the truth. Every layer between you and your team sands the edges off what reaches you. Your stakeholders do it again. By the time it lands on your desk, you're looking at the version everyone agreed was safe to show you, making your hardest calls on your most filtered information.

The problem every cross-functional team shares

Consensus is the slowest decision a launch team makes. Everyone at the table wants to agree. So you meet, and meet again, and the launch waits.

01

Smooth meetings are the warning sign.

The meeting that goes well and settles nothing is the one to worry about. The real objection stays in the room next door.

02

Consensus is how teams avoid deciding.

Global wants one story, the regions want theirs, every function reads the data its own way. So you book the follow-up, fill the calendar, and call the delay “alignment.”

03

By the time you agree, the market has moved.

Your competitor claimed the thought leaders and secured the formulary positions. You ran a careful process. They ran a launch.

You can't lead what you can't see. So the work starts with stakeholder intelligence.  Then we build the plan that gets your team aligned for real. We coach you and your leaders through it, from the first decision to the impact that holds.

You leave with it fixed.
